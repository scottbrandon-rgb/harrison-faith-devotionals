Create a form titled "Young Adult Ministry — Leader Input" with the following introduction text:

"We are building a webpage for people who get invited to the gathering, so they know what they are walking into before they walk in. Answer these the way you would tell a friend, not the way you would write a church bulletin. Specific beats polished. If a question does not apply, say so and move on. Takes about 20 minutes — feel free to dictate your answers instead of typing."

Use a progress bar and split the form into five pages. Set a thank-you message of "Got it. Thank you — this is exactly what we needed."

**Opening field**
- Short text: "Your name" (required)

**Page 1 — The Night Itself**
Page description: "Describe what actually happens, not what it is supposed to mean."
All long-answer text, all required:
1. "Walk me through last week's gathering minute by minute." Sub-label: From the time the first person walked in until the last person left. Include the awkward parts and the dead time.
2. "What does someone walking in for the first time literally see, hear, and smell?" Sub-label: Lights, room setup, music, coffee, whatever is actually there.
3. "Who was in the room?" Sub-label: Ages, life stages, married, single, students, working. Roughly how many.
4. "How long does it run, and what is the actual food and drink situation?"

**Page 2 — What It Is For**
Page description: "Real examples, not mission-statement language."
All long-answer text, all required:
5. "Finish this sentence: People keep coming back because ______."
6. "Tell me about one person whose life is different because of this group. What changed?" Sub-label: No last names needed.
7. "If this group disappeared tomorrow, what would people miss most?"
8. "What do we do here that people cannot get on a Sunday morning?"

**Page 3 — The First-Timer**
Page description: "Most of the webpage is aimed at someone who has never come."
All long-answer text, all required:
9. "What is the number one thing a first-timer is nervous about before they walk in?"
10. "What have people actually said to you after their first night?" Sub-label: Quote them if you can remember the words.
11. "What do we NOT do?" Sub-label: Make anyone pray out loud their first time? Put them on the spot? Pass a plate? Name the things nobody has to worry about.
12. "Who is this honestly not a fit for?" Sub-label: This one feels wrong to answer. Answer it anyway. A page that is for everyone reads like it is for no one.

**Page 4 — Logistics**
Page description: "Short answers are fine here. If you do not know one, write 'not sure.'"
Short text fields, all required:
13. "Where does it meet?" Sub-label: Full address, or building and room.
14. "What day and time?"
15. "How often?" Sub-label: Weekly, every other week, etc.
16. "Does it cost anything?"
17. "Is childcare available? If so, what are the details?"
18. "What do people actually wear?"
19. "Should they bring anything?" Sub-label: Bible, food, nothing.
Long-answer text, required:
20. "Where do people park, and which door do they come in?"
21. "Who does someone text with a question, and how do they RSVP — or do they just show up?" Sub-label: Include the name and phone number or handle we can put on the page.

**Page 5 — Voice**
Page description: "This shapes how the page sounds."
22. Long-answer text, required: "What words or phrases make you cringe when churches use them?" Sub-label: Be blunt. We will keep them off the page.
23. Short text, required: "If you had to describe the night in three words to a friend at work, what three?"
24. Long-answer text, optional: "Anything else we should know?"
