## Devotional
Paul wrote that "we are not ignorant of his designs" (2 Corinthians 2:11), and on Sunday we traced all those designs all the way back to a garden, long before anyone had heard of Plato or Marx. And last week we watched Adam trade his freedom for one bite of fruit. This week we stay in Genesis 3 a little longer. 

Moses tells us the serpent was "more crafty than any other beast of the field" (Genesis 3:1). The Hebrew word for crafty is arum, and one verse earlier the man and his wife were arummim, naked and unashamed (Genesis 2:25). Moses set those 2 words side by side on purpose: the serpent is shrewd, the couple is exposed, and they don't know it yet. His first move is a question, "Did God actually say?" His second move is the lie, "You will be like God" (Genesis 3:5).

Here's what makes it a lie. They were already like God, because "God created man in his own image" (Genesis 1:27). Eve was standing in the garden bearing the image of God when the serpent told her she didn't have it. He took something God had already given her and sold it back to her as something God was keeping from her. That's how discord gets started. Convince someone they've been cheated, and they'll start looking at the One who gave them everything as the one holding out.

Then watch what happens. They eat, and the first thing they do is sew fig leaves to cover themselves (Genesis 3:7), 2 people who had nothing to hide from each other an hour earlier and now can't stand to be seen. Then they hide from God among the trees (Genesis 3:8). Then Adam points at Eve and, in the same breath, at God: "The woman whom you gave to be with me" (Genesis 3:12). In a few verses the man is hiding from his wife, hiding from his God, and blaming them both. Deceive, sin, separation, the same 3 steps we traced all Sunday morning. All the enemy has to do to wreck what God built is get the people inside it to stop trusting each other.

You'll hear that same whisper this week. Somebody got the outcome they wanted last Tuesday and somebody didn't, and the whisper will tell you that you've been cheated, and that the people across the street, across the pew or across the nation are the reason. It sounds like insight. It's the oldest play in the book, and it's in the first book!! Before you agree with the whisper, ask who benefits if you believe it, because we are not ignorant of his designs.

**When the whisper says you've been cheated, check who's whispering.**

## Application
1. Where in your life have you started believing that God, or someone close to you, is holding out on you?
2. Who is one person you've quietly pulled away from that you could call or text this week?

## Challenge
Read Genesis 3 slowly and mark every place someone hides, covers up, or points a finger. Then think back on the last argument you had, at home, at work, or online, and ask whether it moved people closer together or further apart. Before the day is over, take one step back toward someone the whisper has been pushing you away from.

## Prayer
Father, you made me in your image before I ever did a thing to earn it, and I don't need a serpent to tell me what you've given me. Open my eyes to every whisper that says I've been cheated, by you or by the people around me. Where I've been hiding and blaming, bring me back into the open with you and with them. Keep me from being outwitted this week, because I'm not ignorant of his designs. Amen.
