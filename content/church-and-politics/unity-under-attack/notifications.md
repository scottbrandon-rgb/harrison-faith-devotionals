Last week we watched free stuff and the fear of death talk people out of their freedom, and this week we find the enemy's other tool, division. Monday we go back to the garden and hear the first whisper that turned a husband against his wife and both of them against God. Tuesday we stand in Cain's field and find out why it matters that God is always watching. Wednesday we land at Plymouth, where good men learned the hard way that they weren't wiser than God. Thursday we watch Abimelech sort brothers by bloodline and Jotham climb a mountain to speak up. Friday we stand where the wall used to be and worship the One who tore it down.
