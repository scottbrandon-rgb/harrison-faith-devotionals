Last week we saw how the enemy divides us, and this week we close our Church and Politics series with the hardest conversation of the whole series. Monday we hear the alarm going off in Romans 1 and learn what it means when a culture starts applauding sin. Tuesday we trace how America got flooded, from Kinsey to After the Ball, and ask God to make us sensitive again. Wednesday we answer 3 arguments with what Jesus and Paul actually said. Thursday we stand on God's design for marriage and find out why desire needs a home. Friday we end the series in worship, because the God who sounds the alarm is the God who washes sinners clean.
