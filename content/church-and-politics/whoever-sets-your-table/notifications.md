Last week Daniel pushed back from the king's table, and this week we follow that table all the way back to the garden and ask who's feeding you. Monday we sit down at the first table God ever set, before Adam had earned a thing. Tuesday we watch the fear of death sell Egypt to Pharaoh one bag of grain at a time. Wednesday we hear Israel cry for the free fish of Egypt and forget what it cost. Thursday we find out why God let His son go hungry, and what Daniel learned from it in Babylon. Friday we watch Jesus beat free stuff in the wilderness and the fear of death on the cross.
