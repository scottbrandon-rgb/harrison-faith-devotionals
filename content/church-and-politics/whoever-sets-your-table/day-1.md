## Devotional
Everybody in Harrison got an extra hour this weekend, and not one of us did a thing to earn it. The clocks fell back and the hour was handed to us, whether we slept through it or lay there awake at 5 wondering what time it really was. Last week Daniel pushed back from Nebuchadnezzar's table. This week we go back to the very first table God ever set, and it was set the same way that hour came, as a gift before anyone had done a thing to deserve it.

Read Genesis 1 slowly and watch what God hands out in 3 verses. He gave Adam and Eve His own image, "in the image of God he created him" (Genesis 1:27). He gave them a family and told them to "be fruitful and multiply." He gave them dominion over the earth. Then He set the table, "Behold, I have given you every plant yielding seed that is on the face of all the earth... You shall have them for food" (Genesis 1:29). Every promise a government will ever make to you, God made first, and He made it before Adam had worked a single day.

The order matters, because whoever feeds you will eventually try to define you. About 200 years ago the German philosopher Hegel, whose ideas Karl Marx built on, wrote that "all the worth which the human being possesses... he possesses only through the State." Genesis had already answered him. Your worth was stamped on you in the image of God before there was a king, a Pharaoh or a state around to hand it out. Whoever sets your table will try to set your loyalty, and God made sure the first name and the first meal both came from Him.

Look at what the serpent used to pull them away. He told Eve "you will be like God" (Genesis 3:5), and in the very next verse she "saw that the tree was good for food" (Genesis 3:6). Adam could already eat from "every tree of the garden" but one (Genesis 2:16). The temptation was to become his own provider, to set his own table and decide for himself what he would eat. They handed over their dominion and their freedom for one meal, and they walked out of the garden to eat "by the sweat of your face" (Genesis 3:19). Providing for themselves cost them their life, their provision, their dominion and their freedom.

Adam reached for the one thing he didn't have and lost everything he'd been given. You've got a Father who still sets the table, "The eyes of all look to you, and you give them their food in due season" (Psalm 145:15).

**You were named and fed before you earned a thing.**

## Application
1. Where have you been reaching for the one thing God told you to leave alone, as if the rest of His table weren't enough?
2. What is one gift on your table this week you could thank God for out loud before you ask Him for anything else?

## Challenge
Read Genesis 1:26 through 31 before your first meal today. Write down the 4 things God handed Adam and Eve, a name, a family, dominion and food, and next to each one write how He has given you the same. Thank Him for every line before you eat.

## Prayer
Father, you set the first table before Adam ever did a day's work, and you've set mine the same way. Every hour, every meal and every breath I have came from your hand. Forgive me for reaching for the one thing you told me to leave alone, as if the rest of your table weren't enough. Keep my worth and my loyalty anchored in you. Amen.
