Early voting opens Monday, and this week we go up on the wall with Ezekiel. Monday we look at how one body with a lot of different opinions stays one body, and why a quiet church can still be a divided one. Tuesday Ezekiel 33 shows us a watchman who stayed quiet and still answered for the blood. Wednesday we walk into the guild halls of Thyatira, where Jezebel offered a comfortable life in exchange for a voice. Thursday Eli shows us what happens when the right words never turn into action, and we talk about your voice, your table and your vote. Friday we land on the heart behind every warning, a God who takes no pleasure in the death of the wicked and already let judgment fall on His Son.
