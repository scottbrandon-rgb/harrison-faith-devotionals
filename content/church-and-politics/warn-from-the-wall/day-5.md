## Devotional
Every warning in Ezekiel 33 has been leading to verse 11. God swears an oath on His own life, "As I live," and what He swears to is His heart. "I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but that the wicked turn from his way and live." Then He says it twice. In Hebrew it's shuvu, shuvu, turn back, turn back, and when God repeats Himself like that, He's pleading. "For why will you die, O house of Israel?"

That's why the watchman blows the trumpet in the first place. God sends the warning because He wants the people to live. The trumpet is God's love speaking through a man on the wall, warning a city that may not love Him back. The whole week comes down to this, the watchman who warns delivers his own soul, and the God who sends him wants to deliver theirs.

The sword in Ezekiel's day was real, and it came. Judgment is still real, and Scripture never softens it. God has already let it fall, though, on His own Son. "But God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us" (Romans 5:8). Jesus took the sword in our place. The judgment every one of us deserved landed on Him at the cross, and for everyone who has trusted Him, it's finished. Jesus said it this way, "Truly, truly, I say to you, whoever hears my word and believes him who sent me has eternal life. He does not come into judgment, but has passed from death to life" (John 5:24).

So here's the question every watchman eventually puts to the city, and I'm putting it to you today. Is your judgment waiting, or is your judgment paid? If you've received Jesus, there's no judgment waiting for you, only righteousness and peace and the Spirit of His Son in your heart, "crying, 'Abba! Father!'" (Galatians 4:6). If you haven't, the trumpet is sounding right now, and it's sounding because He loves you.

That's what makes the watchman's job worth every hard conversation. We stand on the wall with a message about judgment, and we get to finish it with the best news the world has ever heard. Our God pleads with sinners, our God saves sinners, and He has made sinners like us His mouth to the city. "Therefore, we are ambassadors for Christ, God making his appeal through us" (2 Corinthians 5:20).

**The God who sends the warning wants every listener to live.**

## Application
1. Honestly, is your judgment waiting, or is your judgment paid?
2. Who is one person you could tell this week how Jesus took the judgment they deserve?

## Challenge
Write down the name of one person you know who doesn't know Jesus. Pray Ezekiel 33:11 over them by name today, and then watch for the moment this week to tell them what Jesus has done. Sunday we'll open the Word on what our government and our culture are trying to define, and we'll see what God has already said, so come ready.

Thank you that the judgment I deserved fell on Jesus, and that I have passed from death to life. Break my heart for the people around me the way your heart breaks for them. Make me a watchman who warns with your love in my voice. Amen.
