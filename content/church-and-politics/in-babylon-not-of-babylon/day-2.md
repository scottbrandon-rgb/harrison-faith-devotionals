## Devotional
Daniel spent 3 years in Babylon's school (Daniel 1:5). It was the best education the ancient world had to offer, cuneiform tablets, mathematics, the movement of the stars, and long lists of omens the priests used to read the will of their gods in everything from eclipses to sheep livers. Nebuchadnezzar wanted to fill these young men with Babylonian science, Babylonian religion and Babylonian values until the things of Zion faded out of them. He knew what every empire knows. You change a people by changing what goes into the next generation.

Paul saw that same classroom in his day. "See to it that no one takes you captive by philosophy and empty deceit, according to human tradition" (Colossians 2:8). The Greek word for takes you captive is sylagogeo, and it pictures an army hauling off spoil after a battle. That's what the wrong literature does to a mind. It carries you off while your body stays right where it is. Plenty of people are free in body and captive in mind. Babylon took Daniel captive, and it never captured his mind.

You have a classroom too, and it's open 24 hours a day. Your eyes are a gate and your ears are a gate, and you decide what comes through them. The feed you scroll, the news you leave on, the shows you binge and the podcast in your truck are all teaching you something about right and wrong. Paul gave us the filter, "whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is just, whatever is pure... think about these things" (Philippians 4:8). What goes into you determines who you are. It shapes how you think, and then it shapes how you act.

Daniel held on because he knew God's Word, and it was the one book he measured every other book against. That's what we mean at Harrison Faith when we say we follow one book. Amos saw "the Lord was standing beside a wall built with a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand" (Amos 7:7). A plumb line is a weight on a string, and gravity pulls it straight down every single time, so a builder can hold it beside a wall and see exactly which stone has drifted off the foundation. The first stone out of line becomes the new foundation for every stone stacked on top of it, and the higher the wall goes, the worse the lean gets. When the wall and the foundation disagree, the wall is what has to move.

That's how Babylon moves into a believer, one book at a time, one feed at a time, one crooked stone at a time. All Scripture is "breathed out by God" (2 Timothy 3:16), and everything else you read gets held up against it.

**Measure everything you take in against the one book.**

## Application
1. Honestly, what has been teaching you the most this past week, Scripture or your screen?
2. What is one input you could cut this week to make more room for God's Word?

## Challenge
Read Psalm 119:97 through 104 before you open any app today. Write verse 99 somewhere you'll see it, "I have more understanding than all my teachers, for your testimonies are my meditation." Tonight, check your screen time and compare it honestly with the minutes you spent in the Word.

## Prayer
Lord, your Word is the plumb line, and I want my life measured by it. Show me the places where I've let the world's classroom teach me more than you have. Guard the gates of my eyes and ears, and fill my mind with what is true and pure. Keep me free in body and free in mind. Amen.
