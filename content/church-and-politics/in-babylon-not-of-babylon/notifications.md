Daniel was carried into Babylon as a teenager, and for close to 70 years Babylon never got into him. Monday we see how Babylon went after his language first, and why the words you use shape the way you think. Tuesday we step into Babylon's classroom and hold everything we take in against one book. Wednesday we sit down at the king's table and ask who's really feeding you. Thursday we watch Daniel set his heart in the same breath Babylon set his name, and we'll talk about Sunday's eighth baptism. Friday we land on a Savior who walked into our Babylon to bring us out and sends us back with His own mind.
