## Devotional

Here's the setting, it's Resurrection Sunday, the tomb is already empty, the angels have already spoken, the women have already run back with the report, and two men are walking out of Jerusalem in the opposite direction of the greatest news in human history. Emmaus sat about seven miles out, which is close to a two hour walk at the pace of a conversation. Two hours of a man walking shoulder to shoulder with the resurrected Son of God and not knowing it.

Now watch what Jesus does, because verse 15 holds the most underrated sentence in the chapter. "Jesus himself drew near and went with them." He does not summon them back to the city. He does not send word to Jerusalem to have somebody go fetch them. He does not stand at the gate with His arms crossed waiting on them to come to their senses. He walks out of town after them, and He gets close enough to hear every word. In verse 17 He asks what they have been discussing, though verse 15 already told us He was near enough to hear them. He listened to two men He had personally taught explain to each other why it had not worked, and He kept walking with them anyway.

Then Luke says something most of us read past. "But their eyes were kept from recognizing him" (Luke 24:16). Slow down on that grammar for a minute. It does not say their eyes failed, and it does not say they were too upset to notice. It says their eyes were kept, a passive verb with God standing behind it. On the single greatest day in redemptive history, Jesus deliberately walked with two of His own people in a form they could not identify.

So hear this before you go one step further into your day. 
Your disappointment does not repel Him and your confusion does not offend Him. 
That conversation you had in the car, your room, leaving the job, or walking in the field, saying your done with it all!  Know this, He was close enough to hear it and He is STILL walking with you.  God understands our desire to look for Him in the place we found Him, the familiar place. But God has come to reveal Himself greater to you and greater to me.

**God reveals Himself in greater ways. We look for Him in familiar ones.**

## Application

1. What have you been saying about God lately that you would not want Him to overhear, and what does that reveal about how much you think He can handle?
2. What would change in your next few hours if you walked through them assuming He is already beside you instead of waiting for you to get back to where you left Him?

## Challenge

Name your road out loud to Him today. Tell Him where you have been quietly walking, what you stopped expecting, and the honest thing you have only said to yourself. He already heard it on the road. Saying it to His face is how you stop walking alone with it.

## Prayer

Lord, I have stood in the middle of Your faithfulness and called it abandonment, and I did not even know I was doing it. Thank You that You walk out of the city after people who are walking away, that You get close enough to hear the worst of what we are saying and keep walking anyway. Forgive me for the category I built for You that is too small to hold what You are actually doing. Open my eyes on the road I am already on. In Jesus' name, amen.
