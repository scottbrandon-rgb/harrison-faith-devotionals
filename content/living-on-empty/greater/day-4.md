## Devotional

Start with what just happened, because it ought to unsettle you. Jesus preached them the greatest sermon in human history. Genesis to Malachi, from the mouth of the risen Christ Himself, two hours of the perfect preacher on the perfect text. And at the end of it, in verse 28, they still did not know who He was. Bible study by itself was not enough to open their eyes.

Then comes verse 28. "He acted as if he were going farther." Do not misread that as deception. He is doing what He has always done. He is waiting to be wanted. He did it with Jacob at the Jabbok, wrestling until daybreak and then saying let me go, until Jacob said I will not let you go unless you bless me. He does it in Revelation 3, standing at the door and knocking on a door that only opens from the inside. He will walk with you for seven miles uninvited. He will not sit down at your table uninvited.

Look at the two words that got Him through the door. "Stay with us." In the Greek that is built on the same root John uses when Jesus says abide in Me. It is not enough that we abide in the Word for two hours on a road. The Word has to come in and abide with us, at the table, in the house, where the day ends and the guard comes down.

So here is the question He was asking those two men, and it is the question He is asking somebody reading this. Do you want Me, or do you want information about Me? You can sit under preaching your entire life and never once ask Him to stay. You can take notes on every sermon, listen to the podcast on the drive, know the outline before your pastor preaches it, and have Him walk right on past your house at sundown because nobody ever asked Him in. Knowing about Him will never be the same as knowing Him, and Paul counted everything he had as loss for the sake of the second one (Philippians 3:8).

Notice too that they were not asked to have it all figured out first. Their theology was still a wreck when they said stay with us. Their hope was still past tense. They had heard two hours of preaching they could not yet connect to the man standing in front of them, and He went in anyway. Bible study has to become fellowship or you will spend your whole life walking beside a Jesus you never recognize.

**Information will let Him walk beside you. Invitation will get Him through your door.**

## Application

1. Honestly, do you want to be informed about Christ or connected to Him, and which one have your last few weeks actually been built for?
2. Where in your day is there a table, a real one with time and quiet around it, that you could invite Him into this week?

## Challenge

Ask Him in today, out loud, in the room where you actually live. Not the drive time and not the podcast, the table. Say stay with me and then stay there long enough for it to be fellowship instead of study. You do not have to have your theology sorted out first. Those two men certainly did not.

## Prayer

Jesus, I have walked beside You for years and called it knowing You. I have taken the notes, learned the outlines, and never once asked You to come in and stay. Forgive me for settling for information about You when You were waiting the whole time to be wanted. I am asking now, with my theology still a wreck and my hope still limping. Stay with me. Come to the table. In Your name, amen.
