WEEK OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Two men walked seven miles out of Jerusalem on Resurrection Sunday, in the opposite direction of the greatest news in human history, with the risen Christ walking beside them the whole way. They did not recognize Him, and not because they were faithless. Their hope had been fixed on a redemption smaller than the one God was actually accomplishing. This week we walk that road with them, from a hope moved into the past tense, to the whole Bible reinterpreting the worst seventy two hours of their lives, to a table where their eyes finally opened and a fire that had gone out caught again. If you have been grieving a season and calling it grieving God, this week is for you.
