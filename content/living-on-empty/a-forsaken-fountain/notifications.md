This week we're in Jeremiah 2:13, where God levels a double charge at His own people. They walked away from the fountain of living waters, and then they went out and dug their own cracked holes in the ground to replace Him. Pastor Caleb opened it Sunday while Pastor Scott recovers, and we're taking it one step a day. We'll sit in the Hebrew word for abandon, stand in an ancient town that called its spring the eye of the place, look honestly at the substitutes we've been plastering, and end at a well at noon with a woman Jesus invited before He ever mentioned her past. If you've been running on empty, this week is for you.

Day 2 — The Priests Stopped Asking

The fountain never shut down, never dried up, and never moved, so let's go back to it together. See you in the devos.
