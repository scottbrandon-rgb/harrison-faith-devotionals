## Devotional

Ancient Israel had almost no permanent rivers. Rain came for part of the year and then quit for months at a stretch, and everything depended on somebody finding a spring that kept flowing when the sky went quiet. Towns were not built where the view was good. Towns were built on top of the water, because water decided whether there would be a town at all.

Here is the detail that has stayed with me all week. The Hebrew word *ayin* means eye. It also means spring. One word, both things, and the ancient Hebrew and Arab speakers used it that way on purpose. A spring was the eye of the locality. The eye of the town. That is how central it was to survival and to identity, and spring water was the finest and purest water they had access to, which is why they read a flowing spring as a sign of God's favor on that place.

Hold that word in your hand and go back to Jeremiah. "They have forsaken me, the fountain of living waters." God chose the strongest picture that culture had available. When Israel walked away from Him, they tore out their own eye. Then they went off into ground that had never held water in its life and started digging. Blind, dry, empty, and convinced they were being resourceful.

That's the whole diagnosis of this week. What we build cannot retain what it promises. Israel had access to the purest water in the region and swapped it for a hole in the dirt, and before you shake your head at them, look at what you have been drawing from lately. Because you will only ever see as clearly as your source allows. "For with you is the fountain of life; in your light do we see light."

There is no second fountain. Isaiah put it about as plainly as language allows: "Turn to me and be saved, all the ends of the earth! For I am God, and there is no other" (Isaiah 45:22). One fountain. One way. One source. And He is still the eye of the place where you are standing today.

**What you drink from decides what you can see.**

## Application

1. Where have you been getting your sense of how life is going, honestly, over the last month?
2. What would it look like to go to the Lord first with the thing you usually take to somebody else?

## Challenge

Take the first ten minutes of your day tomorrow and give them to Scripture and prayer before anything else touches your eyes. No phone, no email, no news. Do it once and pay attention to how differently you see the rest of the day.

## Prayer

Lord, You are the fountain of life, and I have been drinking from ground that never held water. I have gone half blind and called it being practical. Be the eye of my life again, the center of my survival and my identity, and let me see everything else by Your light. There is no other, and I am done pretending there is. Amen.
