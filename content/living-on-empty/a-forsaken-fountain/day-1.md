## Devotional

I want to walk you back through what we opened that morning, one step a day. An altar moment is a good start. A week of sitting with the text is how it really takes root.

Remember when Jonah prayed this from inside a fish: "Those who pay regard to vain idols forsake their hope of steadfast love" (Jonah 2:8). Solomon reached for that same idea after he had gotten everything a man could want. The Hebrew word he kept coming back to is *hevel*. Vapor. Mist. Breath on a cold morning. You can see it and chase it, but you cannot hold it. "Vanity of vanities, says the Preacher, vanity of vanities! All is vanity" (Ecclesiastes 1:2). The wisest and wealthiest man in the ancient world chased down every offer this life makes and came back with wet hands and nothing in them. If Solomon could identify the emptiness of the things we think fill us, we should listen.

Here is what I could not get past while I was preparing. We already know. 
Nobody has to convince you the promotion won't fill you. 
Nobody has to prove to you that the follower count won't fill you, or that the person you keep waiting on won't fill you. 
You have watched all of it evaporate before, in your own hands, more than once. **And you reach for it again anyway!**

That's why Jeremiah 2:13 reads like a diagnosis. "My people have committed two evils." A double evil, and He says it about the people who carry His name. This is a family conversation. The pattern repeated in every generation after Jeremiah, which means your name sits under that diagnosis too.

We'll get to the second evil later this week. But think on the first one today, *because you cannot fix what you refuse to look at*. Something living was offered to you, and you or maybe we, traded it for vapor.

**Name the one thing you're holding that you already know won't hold.**

## Application

1. Where in your life have you kept reaching for something you already watched slip through your hands?
2. What is one thing you could stop pouring energy into this week so there's something left for the Lord?

## Challenge

Look honestly at where your energy actually went last week. Your calendar and your bank statement will tell you faster than your memory will. Write down the three things that took the most out of you, then put a mark beside the one you already know is vapor.

## Prayer

Lord, I have been holding things and trusting in things that were never able to hold me. I have spent myself on what evaporates and then wondered why I feel dry. Show me today what I am gripping that You never handed me. You are the fountain of living waters, and I am asking You to be my source and not my backup plan. Amen.
