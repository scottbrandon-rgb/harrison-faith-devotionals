## Devotional

She came to the well alone, and the timing tells you everything. Water was drawn in the cool of the morning, together, in a crowd of women. She came when the sun was highest and the well was empty of people, which is what you do when you have arranged your whole life around not being seen.

Watch the order of what Jesus does, because this is the part I cannot get over. He asks her for a drink. He tells her about living water. He offers her a spring that wells up inside a person forever. And only after all of that does He say, "Go, call your husband, and come here" (John 4:16). The invitation came first. Her brokenness came second. He handled her thirst before He ever touched her history.

You do not have to come to the fountain whole. You come as you are, thirsty and cracked and carrying the noon hour, and you let the living fountain do the healing you were trying to finish before you showed up. Every one of us has been waiting to get cleaned up before we get close. He never asked for that.

Then look at where the whole story lands. Jeremiah watched a people forsake the fountain. Centuries later Zechariah stood in the same prophetic vocabulary and turned it completely around: "On that day there shall be a fountain opened for the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to cleanse them from sin and uncleanness" (Zechariah 13:1). A fountain forsaken in one prophet. A fountain opened in the other. That's the gospel in two words of Hebrew, and it cost God everything to write the second one.

So here we are at the end of the week, back where we started, with vapor in our hands and a fountain in front of us. Jesus said, "I came that they may have life and have it abundantly" (John 10:10). He is not offering you a fuller schedule or a better version of your effort. He is offering you Himself, flowing, unlimited, still available on a Friday, still the eye of the place where you are standing. The fountain never shut down. It has been waiting on you the whole time.

**Come thirsty and come today, because the fountain never asked you to arrive clean.**

## Application

1. What have you been waiting to fix about yourself before you get close to God again?
2. Who is one person you could tell this week that the fountain is still open?

## Challenge

Go to God today without cleaning anything up first. Bring the exact thing you have been hiding from Him at noon, say it plainly, and stay there long enough to drink. Then thank Him for a fountain you did nothing to open.

## Prayer

Jesus, I have been arranging my life around not being seen, and You met me at the well anyway. You asked me for a drink before You said a word about my mess. Thank You that the fountain was opened at Your cost and not mine, and that it has never once run dry. I came empty today, and I am leaving full because You are the source and there is no other. Amen.
