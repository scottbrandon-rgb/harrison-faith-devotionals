## Devotional

The word behind "forsaken" in Jeremiah 2:13 is *azav*. It means to leave, to abandon, to withdraw. *Azav* is the feeling of a slow drift or lingering that stretches longer than it should, and before you know it, you realize how long it’s been since you left.

Back up five verses and look at who He is talking to, it's the priests. The men who handled the law. The shepherds. The prophets. These were the credentialed ones, the people with the schedule and the vestments and the platform, and the indictment against them is almost embarrassing in how small it is: "The priests did not say, 'Where is the LORD?'" They stopped asking the question. They kept the machinery running and lost the pursuit that made the machinery mean anything.

When you stop pursuing the source, you stop reflecting the source. That's the whole verse in one line. A prophet with no fresh word starts borrowing one, and Jeremiah says these men ended up prophesying by Baal and chasing after things that do not profit. That's the drift. It never begins with rebellion. It begins with a question you used to ask and don't ask anymore.

Now notice what the text refuses to say. The fountain did not shut down. It did not dry up. It did not relocate. Only one party in this relationship moved, and it wasn't God. Jesus said the same thing to a church that looked healthy on paper: "But I have this against you, that you have abandoned the love you had at first" (Revelation 2:4). Same charge, same word family, same slow walk to the door.

So let me ask you what the Lord asked me while I was writing this. How can you live a life that stays full if you are not walking closely with the only fountain that can keep you full? You cannot pour out of a well you quit visiting. Everything you are handing other people right now is coming from somewhere. The only question is where.

**Ask the question the priests quit asking: where is the Lord in this?**

## Application

1. When did you last ask where the Lord was in something before you asked how you were going to handle it?
2. What is one place in your week where you could put pursuit back in front of performance?

## Challenge

Pick the one responsibility you are currently carrying that feels the heaviest. Before you touch it again today, stop and ask out loud where the Lord is in it, and then wait long enough to actually listen. Do that before you open the laptop, not after.

## Prayer

Father, I have kept the routine going long after I quit pursuing You inside of it. I did not walk out on You in a moment, I drifted a step at a time, and I am the one who moved. Teach me to ask where You are before I ask what I should do. You have not shut down and You have not gone anywhere, so I am turning back around today. Amen.
