This week we are walking through how a cistern actually gets dug, one step at a time, out of Judges 1 and 2. Israel moved from a land they could irrigate to a land that had to be rained on, and they spent the next generation trying to get back to something they could control. It starts with a Savior we can look to and a solution we can craft, and it ends with altars nobody wants to tear down. Nobody wakes up and decides to forsake God. We just keep finding things that work without Him, and every one of them has a handle we can turn.
