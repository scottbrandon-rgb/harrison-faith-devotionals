## Devotional

As she poured, they brought the vessels to her (2 Kings 4:5). Did you notice the "they?"

She wasn't in that room by herself.

Her sons were on the other end of the whole thing. She poured, they carried. And they weren't neutral helpers doing their mother a favor, because they were the ones the creditor was coming to take. Every vessel they set down in front of her was a bid for their own freedom.

The door was shut to the crowd. It was never shut to them.

Pastor Caleb gave us 3 words on this Sunday and warned us we weren't ready for them. Let people in.

Then he narrowed it, which is the part that keeps it from turning into bad advice. He isn't telling you to walk into Walmart, find a stranger, and hand them your whole story. He's talking about the people God has already placed in this body on purpose, the ones you do life with, the ones who will walk with you instead of talk about you.

So how do you tell the difference? Pastor Caleb put it about like this. Faithful people will watch you follow instructions that look ridiculous and they'll recognize it for what it is, because faithful people draw faithful people. Unfaithful people will watch that same obedience and call it foolishness.

There's your filter. Watch how somebody responds to your obedience while it still doesn't make sense.

Solomon said 2 are better than 1, "For if they fall, one will lift up his fellow. But woe to him who is alone when he falls and has not another to lift him up!" (Ecclesiastes 4:10). The trouble is that most of us don't find out we've been alone until we're already on the ground.

Then Pastor Caleb got specific, and this is where it landed hardest.

He said that while he was writing the message, he sensed the Lord telling him that a lot of marriages in this church are running on empty because we haven't been faithful with hidden obedience at home. Then he gave us a question to write down, and he was careful about how he framed it. You don't ask it about your wife. You don't ask it about your husband.

Lord, in what area are you asking me to be faithful with hidden obedience in my marriage?

He also said, before anybody could take it the wrong way, that he doesn't have a perfect marriage and he wasn't holding his up as the model.

Think about what those boys actually did in that room. They didn't pour, and they couldn't have. All they could do was keep setting empty things in front of their mother and trust her to fill them, and the oil ran as long as they kept coming.

Somebody in your life is willing to do that for you. You've just never let them close enough to hand you anything.

**Let 1 faithful person into the thing you've been carrying alone.**

## Application

1. Where have you been calling it privacy when it's really been pride?
2. Who is one person you could ask this week to walk with you in something God has told you to do?

## Challenge

Think of the person who has proven faithful to you and doesn't need every detail to stay. Tell them one thing God has asked you to do and ask them to check on you about it. If you're married, ask Pastor Caleb's question about yourself before this week is over and write down what comes back.

## Prayer

God, I've been doing this alone and calling it strength. I've kept the door shut on people You put in my life on purpose, and I told myself it was privacy when the truth is I didn't want anybody seeing me empty. Show me who You've already given me. Give me enough humility to hand them something and let them carry it a while. And in my own house, show me where You're asking me to be faithful when nobody is looking.
