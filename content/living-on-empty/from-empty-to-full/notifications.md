Pastor Caleb took us to 2 Kings 4 Sunday, to a widow whose husband was dead, whose debt was still owed, and whose 2 sons were about to be taken as payment. She had one small flask of oil and nothing else in the house, and what God did with that flask is the whole point of this week. Pastor Caleb asked a question I haven't been able to put down since: what if the emptiness you're carrying is evidence that you're standing in the middle of a miracle? Your emptiness isn't proof God forgot you, it's room for Him to move. This week we walk the 4 postures that carried this woman from empty to full, and we finish on the one thing that stopped the oil in her house.
