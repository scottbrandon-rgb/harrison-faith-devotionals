For 3 weeks I have been telling you the empty was not your fault, and I meant every word of it. This week we handle the other half of that. Jonah did everything God told him to do and still ended up sitting outside the city wanting to die, and the reason had nothing at all to do with how much he was doing. There was something in his hand he loved more than what God loved. We are going to walk 5 days through that, and it is going to get personal before it gets comfortable.

Day 1 — The Bill He Owed
Day 2 — The Complaint He Filed
Day 3 — What Your Hand Holds
Day 4 — So Long, Brown Gum
Day 5 — Right Hand From Left

Bring an open hand this week and we will deal with it together. See you in the devos.

DAILY NOTIFICATIONS (140 characters each)

Day 1 — The Bill He Owed
You did everything God asked of you this week. What was moving your feet while you did it?

Day 2 — The Complaint He Filed
You love that God is merciful. Do you still love it when He is merciful to the one person you cannot stand?

Day 3 — What Your Hand Holds
Nobody took God's love away from you. What did you turn loose of it to keep holding?

Day 4 — So Long, Brown Gum
When is the last time you obeyed God and actually wanted to?

Day 5 — Right Hand From Left
God is delighted in somebody you wrote off. How long has that name been sitting on your list?
