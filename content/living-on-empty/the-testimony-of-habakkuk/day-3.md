## Devotional

Right before Habakkuk tells the earth to be quiet, he walks you through a workshop. "What profit is an idol when its maker has shaped it, a metal image, a teacher of lies? For its maker trusts in his own creation when he makes speechless idols!" (Habakkuk 2:18).

Understand what he's describing, because it was the most ordinary thing in the world to a Babylonian. A craftsman carved the god. Then the priests carried it through a ceremony where they washed and opened its mouth, so the thing could finally eat, and breathe, and speak. The god could not say one word until the people who made it gave it a mouth.

That's the arrangement you signed up for. You make it, you gild it, you carry it, you prop it up when it tips over, and then you speak on its behalf, because it has no breath in it at all (2:19). Isaiah watched the same thing and said they "lift it to their shoulders, they carry it, they set it in its place, and it stands there; it cannot move from its place" (Isaiah 46:7). Every god you build ends up riding on you.

Then verse 20 lands like a door closing. "But the LORD is in his holy temple; let all the earth keep silence before him."

Notice which direction the silence runs. The idols can't talk, so their people have to do the talking. Our God tells the whole earth to hush. He doesn't need you to brief Him on the situation, or lay out the timeline, or walk Him through the options, or total up what it's going to cost. He's in the temple, and in the temple there's a throne, and He is seated on it. The One who sees everything and can do anything, sitting down.

Sunday morning something happened in that room. God came down and was among us, and you don't manufacture that or schedule it. Which is exactly the point Habakkuk is making. You went to the temple and God met you there because He was already there, already seated, already ruling over the very thing that had you undone all week.

So what do you do in a temple? You worship in it. In your confusion you worship. In your emptiness you worship. In your worst week of the year, with nothing solved and nothing explained, you worship, because worship is the only appropriate response to a God who's already on the throne.

Some of you have been up half the night trying to think your way out of something you can't fix. God knows how to tell you what He wants you to know. If He hasn't told you yet, go to sleep.

**Stop briefing God on a situation He is already seated over.**

## Application

1. When you pray, how much of it is you explaining the situation to God, and how much is you worshiping the God who already sees it?
2. What would it look like to sit down for 5 minutes today and say nothing to Him at all?

## Challenge

Find a quiet spot today and give God 5 minutes with no requests in it. No update on the situation, no list, no timeline. Just tell Him what He's like, and then be quiet long enough that it gets uncomfortable.

## Prayer

Lord, I've been carrying things that were supposed to carry me. I built them, I keep them standing, and I've been doing all the talking for both of us. You're in Your holy temple and You're seated, and You've known about this longer than I have. Teach me to be quiet in front of You without needing the answer first. I'll worship in the confusion, because You're worth it there too.
