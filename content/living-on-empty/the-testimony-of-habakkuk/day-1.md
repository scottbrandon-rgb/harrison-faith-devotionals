## Devotional

You're taking too long. "O LORD, how long shall I cry for help, and you will not hear?" (Habakkuk 1:2). Then, I can't figure out what You're doing, and the little You told me makes it worse, because You said You were raising up the Babylonians to discipline us (1:5). Then, what I do understand doesn't look fair, since Your eyes are too pure to look at evil and You're using people worse than me to deal with me (1:13). And finally, I'm afraid (3:2).

Look at what it did to his body. "I hear, and my body trembles; my lips quiver at the sound; rottenness enters into my bones; my legs tremble beneath me" (3:16). That's a man unraveling, and he's the preacher, the one whose whole job is to stand up front and tell everybody else it's going to be all right.

I know what this church is carrying right now. This community has lost 2 students in a little more than a week. There are marriages that are hanging by a thread, health reports nobody wants to open, and families getting hit spiritually, physically, mentally, and financially all at once. It leaves you empty in a places you can't describe.

So what do people actually do with that? The world hands you 3 options:

Resignation says that's just the way it is. It was that way, it is that way, it's going to be that way, so you quit expecting anything to change. You can hear it in somebody, because they sigh more than they talk. Resignation surrenders before the story is finished.

Detachment says distract me so I don't have to think about it. Turn on the television, fill the calendar, pour something, stay busy enough and the ache goes quiet. Detachment is camouflage, and camouflage has never once removed the thing it covered. It's still under there, and you know it.

Bravado says take it like a man. Chin up, positive thinking, my mama raised me to handle anything. Pep talk holds until the score gets bad enough, and then all the pep talk in the world can't reach you. Even the religious kind runs out about 5 minutes after the benediction.

Habakkuk does the one thing none of those 3 will let you do. He speaks to God and then he stands there and waits. David did it the same way in Psalm 13, "How long, O LORD? Will you forget me forever?" and 5 verses later, "I will sing to the LORD."

## Application

1. Where in your life have you been handing God the cleaned up version of how you actually feel?
2. What is one honest thing you could say to God today that you have only ever said to yourself?

## Challenge

Take 10 minutes today with nobody else around and tell God the complaint you've been editing. Use the words you actually use, out loud, and don't soften it on the way out of your mouth. Then stay there long enough to say one true thing you still know about Him.

## Prayer

Lord, I've been telling everybody it's going to be all right while I'm coming apart on the inside. My body knows what my mouth won't say. I'm tired of waiting, I don't understand what You're doing, and some of this doesn't look fair to me. I'm bringing You the real version today instead of the presentable one. And I'm not going anywhere, because You're the only One I have to bring it to.
