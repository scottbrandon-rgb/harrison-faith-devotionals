We have spent this series on why the tank runs dry, and this week we go sit with a prophet who was tired, confused, angry about what wasn't fair, and flat out afraid, and who said all 4 of those things straight to God with his own name on the cover of the book. Habakkuk starts in a pit and ends on a peak, and God never answers a single one of his questions along the way. What he gets instead is God, and by the last verse that turns out to be the thing he was actually after. Given what this church is carrying right now, I don't know that there are 3 better verses in Scripture for us than the ones we land on at the end of this week.

Day 1 — The Preacher Came Apart
You keep telling everybody it is going to be all right. When did you last tell God how it actually is?

Day 2 — Faith Has An Object
You keep asking God for more faith. How much do you actually know about the God you are aiming it at?

Day 3 — He Is Already Seated
How much of your praying is you explaining the situation to a God who is already seated over it?

Day 4 — Suppose Comes Inside
What is the suppose you rehearse at 2 in the morning, and how does that sentence end?

Day 5 — The Mountain Under You
You have been praying that the mountain would move. What if God would rather change your feet?
