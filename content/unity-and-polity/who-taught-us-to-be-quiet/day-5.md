## Devotional
The music started on the plain of Dura, and an entire empire hit the ground. Nebuchadnezzar had set up a golden image 90 feet tall and commanded everyone gathered there to fall down and worship it the moment they heard the horn. In the 6th century B.C., kings did not allow their subjects to believe something different than they did. Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego stayed on their feet and answered that "our God whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace... But if not, be it known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods or worship the golden image that you have set up" (Daniel 3:17-18).

The government always wants to dictate beliefs to people. When King Saul ordered his guard to kill the priests of the LORD at Nob, "the servants of the king would not put out their hand" (1 Samuel 22:17). Doeg the Edomite did it for him. He surrendered his conscience to the government, and 85 priests died that day. Saul's message was the same one Babylon preached, "Don't listen to God, listen to me." If the government says there's more than one gender, there is. If the government says you're a birthing person instead of a mom, you are. It becomes the new reality, unless we refuse to be silent. Nebuchadnezzar is still blowing the trumpet, and people are still bowing.

Babylon's furnace was heated 7 times hotter than usual, so hot that the flames killed the soldiers who threw the 3 men in. Then the king jumped to his feet. "But I see four men unbound, walking in the midst of the fire, and they are not hurt; and the appearance of the fourth is like a son of the gods" (Daniel 3:25). The men who refused to bow had company in the fire. God had already promised it through Isaiah, "When you walk through fire you shall not be burned, and the flame shall not consume you" (Isaiah 43:2).

Look at who walked out. "The hair of their heads was not singed, their cloaks were not harmed, and no smell of fire had come upon them" (Daniel 3:27). The king who demanded worship stood there and said, "Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego," and confessed that "there is no other god who is able to rescue in this way" (3:28-29). Those 3 men refused to bow and refused to keep quiet about why, and the most powerful man on earth ended up preaching about their God. That's the God we serve, and He goes with you into every room where standing up will cost you something. "Our God comes; he does not keep silence" (Psalm 50:3), and the people who belong to Him will not be silent either.

**The God in the fire is worth standing up for.**

## Application
1. Honestly, where have you been bowing to what everyone around you bows to because standing up felt too costly?
2. What would change this week if you actually believed God would be in the fire with you when you speak?

## Challenge
Read all of Daniel 3 out loud today. When you get to verse 25, stop and thank God that He walks into the fire with His people. Then come to church Sunday ready, because that's when we open the Word on government together and I want you in the room.
