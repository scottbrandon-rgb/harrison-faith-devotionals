November 3rd is Election Day, and for the next 3 Sundays we're going to open the Word on the things a lot of people would rather pastors leave alone. This week gets our hearts ready. Monday we start in Acts 4, where Peter and John were ordered to be quiet and told the council they could not stop speaking. Tuesday we go to Numbers 30 and the rule that silence is consent. Wednesday we answer the question in this week's title, who taught us to be quiet, and meet 2 pastor brothers whose names are tied to the Bill of Rights. Thursday James asks whether knowing the right thing and not doing it is sin, and Friday we stand in the fire with 3 men who would not bow.
