6 deaths in 6 weeks will wear a church down, and a worn church divides easier than a rested one. So this week we are guarding what we already have. We start with the enemy's actual strategy in Daniel, spend two days on what Scripture says makes us one body, count the benefits that come with belonging, and finish where Jesus finished, praying that our oneness would tell this city who sent him.
