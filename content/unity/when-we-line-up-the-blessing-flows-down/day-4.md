## Devotional
Paul gives the wives three verses and then turns and spends nine on the husbands, which ought to tell every man reading this where the weight actually sits. "Husbands, love your wives, as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her" (Ephesians 5:25). Then he sharpens the comparison and says it again. "In the same way husbands should love their wives as their own bodies" (Ephesians 5:28). In the same way. He is measuring you against Christ, and that is the whole standard.

**Your life is the ceiling of your expectation.** You and I are never to expect from our wives what our own lives have not already exemplified. If you want her forgiving, go practice forgiving. If you want her respectful, go practice respect. If you want her seeking the Lord with everything she has, then get up and do it first where she can see you do it. A man who has built no example has no ceiling to point at, and the expectation he is carrying around the house is his own invention.

**Her performance doesn't set the temperature of your love.** This is where it gets hard, because when she has been sharp with you all week the flesh wants to withhold, and withholding feels like justice. Christ never waited for the church to behave before he gave himself up for her. So even if she is a mouthy woman, you are called to love her like she is perfect, because that is precisely what was done for you.

**You're never lining up under a person, you're lining up under Christ through a person.** Your wife is going through you to get to the Lord. That sentence ought to land hard on a man, because it means when you are out of line, you are standing in the road she has to walk. Your job is to align with the King, and when you do, the blessing God intends for that marriage runs down through you and lands on her and on the children.

**And when they are better than your example, call it what it is.** I prayed over this myself and found things in my wife and in my kids that I do not do well. Here is what that means. When your family walks in something you never modeled for them, that is grace, plain and simple. God handed them something you did not earn on their behalf. Take the encouragement, and then ask the obvious question. If he will do that around you, what would he do through you if you got in line?

**The workplace runs on the same principle.** "Bondservants, obey your earthly masters with fear and trembling, with a sincere heart, as you would Christ" (Ephesians 6:5). As you would Christ. He says the same thing to the Colossians. "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men" (Colossians 3:23). Most bosses are not against you, they are just for themselves, and you happen to be standing between them and what they want. Your work goes up to the Lord before it ever reaches their desk, which means you already know who is actually signing off on it.

**Employers, you are over people and under someone at the same time.** I keep what I call a head of the table meeting. I walk into the sanctuary with the practical things, the decisions about this ministry or that one, and I sit down at the side of the table because the Lord is at the head of it. I can only be as good an employer as I am a servant to Christ. My ability to see over is tied directly to my ability to sit under. A person who has never been under anyone has no idea what he is asking of the people under him.

That is the chain, and every link in it runs the same direction. The wife through the husband, the child through the parent, the worker through the employer, and every one of those heads through Christ. Nobody in that chain is the destination.

**A man's example is the only expectation he has a right to.**

## Application
1. What are you expecting from your wife, your children, or your employees that your own life has never once demonstrated?
2. What is one thing you could do this week to be the example instead of the enforcement?

## Challenge
Name the standard you have been holding over somebody in your house or at your job. Then go practice that exact thing in front of them today, without pointing it out and without asking whether they noticed. If it is forgiveness, forgive something. If it is respect, give some away where it is not deserved.

## Prayer
Lord, I have been asking for what I have never modeled, and I have called that leadership. Forgive me for standing in the road my family has to walk to get to you. Make my life the example before it is ever an expectation, and where your grace has already covered what I failed to teach them, thank you for it. Put me back in line so what you have for them can reach them. Amen.
