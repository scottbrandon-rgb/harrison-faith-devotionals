## Devotional
Aaron had a job nobody else in Israel could do, and he did it facing two directions at once. When he walked into the Holy Place he carried the nation before God. When he walked back out he carried God before the nation. He was the representative, and everything the people received came through the man standing at the front.

**Aaron is the shadow. Jesus is the reality.** The writer of Hebrews says it without any hedging. "Since then we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus, the Son of God, let us hold fast our confession" (Hebrews 4:14). Then he ties the two men together by name. "And no one takes this honor for himself, but only when called by God, just as Aaron was. So also Christ did not exalt himself to be made a high priest, but was appointed by him" (Hebrews 5:4-5). Aaron never promoted himself into that room, and neither did Jesus. Both were appointed, and appointment is the first shape alignment ever takes.

**He learned obedience.** Here's the verse worth slowing down over. "Although he was a son, he learned obedience through what he suffered. And being made perfect, he became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey him" (Hebrews 5:8-9). Learned obedience is another way of saying he lived in alignment with the Father's will, and the suffering is where that alignment got tested in public. Because he submitted himself, he became the source. The head of all blessing. And that salvation goes to everyone who obeys him, which is the same principle running right back down to you.

**Whatever the head receives, the body receives.** "And he is the head of the body, the church" (Colossians 1:18). There's a flow built into that sentence. So the fair question on a Tuesday morning is what exactly is in the flow, and Scripture answers it five times over.

**Life.** Psalm 133 ends on it, life forevermore, and that is the first thing coming down.

**Blessing.** "Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places" (Ephesians 1:3). Every one of them. If you can find it in the book, it belongs to you, and that includes the ones condemnation has kept you from claiming.

**Authority.** "And he put all things under his feet and gave him as head over all things to the church" (Ephesians 1:22). We are the feet of Christ. We are the hands of Christ. So if a thing sits under his feet, it sits under the body he attached those feet to, and that reaches your marriage, your neighborhood, and the work you walked away from angry on Friday.

**Power.** "And he called the twelve together and gave them power and authority over all demons and to cure diseases" (Luke 9:1). The word there is cure. There are people in our church who would stand up and tell you what that word has done in their body.

**The Spirit.** This is the greatest thing in the flow. The Spirit came down on Jesus like a dove at his baptism (Matthew 3:16), and he did not step into public ministry until it did. Then, exalted at the right hand of God, "having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, he has poured out this that you yourselves are seeing and hearing" (Acts 2:33). The head received it first, and the body got it at Pentecost, because a body cannot do the work of the head without what the head was handed.

**The body is still carrying it.** Paul tells the Thessalonians that "the mystery of lawlessness is already at work. Only he who now restrains it will do so until he is out of the way" (2 Thessalonians 2:7). Whatever you think of the news this week, you have never seen lawlessness with the brakes off. What is holding it back is the Holy Spirit working through the church, and when the church is taken out of the way, the restrainer goes with her. The thing standing between this world and the worst of it came down on the head first.

So the flow is real and the flow is full. Life, blessing, authority, power, and the Spirit himself. All that's left to settle is whether you are standing under it.

**Whatever the Head receives, the body receives, and you are the body.**

## Application
1. Which of those five, life, blessing, authority, power, or the Spirit, have you quietly decided was meant for somebody else?
2. What would you do differently today if you actually believed the authority of Christ reached your situation?

## Challenge
Pick the one situation you have been praying about the longest with the least movement. Your job, a child, a diagnosis, a debt. Today, instead of asking God to send something new, thank him out loud for what the Head already received and speak to that situation as someone standing under it.

## Prayer
Jesus, you were appointed and you learned obedience, and everything the Father gave you came through a life submitted to him. Thank you for being the source. I've been asking you for things you already poured out, so open my eyes to what is already mine because I belong to you. Make me a body that acts like it has a Head. Amen.
