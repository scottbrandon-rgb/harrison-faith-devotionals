## Devotional
Hebrews hands you two instructions about the people who lead you, and they sit ten verses apart. First this. "Remember your leaders, those who spoke to you the word of God. Consider the outcome of their way of life, and imitate their faith" (Hebrews 13:7). Then this. "Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they are keeping watch over your souls, as those who will have to give an account. Let them do this with joy and not with groaning, for that would be of no advantage to you" (Hebrews 13:17). Somebody is losing sleep over your soul and will answer to God for how he handled it.

You align through me, and the preposition is carrying everything. Christ is the head and every leader he gives you is a road to him. That is the difference between a covering and a cage. A covering is a channel, and the whole reason it exists is so something can move through it and reach you.

Paul told the Thessalonians to "respect those who labor among you and are over you in the Lord and admonish you" (1 Thessalonians 5:12). Admonish means they tell you what you need instead of what you want. I know I am not a cheerleader, and you figured that out a long time ago. The word confronts me first, and whatever it requires of me is what I am going to require of you.

If the head isn't aligned, there's nothing to flow down, and that cuts toward the pulpit before it ever cuts toward the pew. The moment I step out of alignment with the Lord I forfeit every ounce of authority I have, and so does any other leader in any other house. So pray for me. I prayed it out loud Sunday and I will put it in writing here, that if I ever block what God intends for this church, he would move me out quickly and mercifully, for your sake and not for mine.

Dads, your family needs to watch you do this. When your wife and your children see you submit to spiritual authority inside a church, you have taught them alignment without ever giving them a lecture on it.

There is a tension in your chest right now and it deserves a straight answer, because America raised us on independence and every one of us feels the pull of it. Here is the answer. Every created head is itself under a Head. "There is no authority except from God, and those that exist have been instituted by God" (Romans 13:1). A president, a policeman, a pastor, or a parent, every one of them holds authority on loan. So when a created head tells you to go against the Head of creation, Peter settled this for us a long time ago. "We must obey God rather than men" (Acts 5:29). A leader who steps out of alignment with God's word has forfeited his claim on your obedience in that thing, and the Head of creation wins every time.

Now go back to the oil. It came down on one head, ran the length of the beard, hit the collar, and covered every name a high priest carried over his heart. Aaron has been dead three thousand years and the stones on that breastpiece are scattered, and yet the Head is still anointed and the oil is still running. "For there the LORD has commanded the blessing, life forevermore" (Psalm 133:3). That verb is a command. He ordered the blessing to a place, and the place is where his people stand together underneath his Son.

So get under the head. Get your marriage under it, get your children under it, get your work under it, get this church under it, and then hold still and let it run. He has already poured it out. He poured it out on a Son who learned obedience, and everything that Son received is coming down over every name he carries.

**When we line up, the blessing flows down.**

## Application
1. Who has God placed over you that you have been aligning to instead of aligning through?
2. What would change in your home this week if your family watched you submit to something you did not have to?

## Challenge
Pray for the leaders God has put over you by name today, starting with the ones you find hardest to follow. Then pick the one place you have been out of line all week, your marriage, your parents, your job, or this church, and take the first step back under it before the weekend. Tell one person you did it so it stays done.

## Prayer
Father, you commanded the blessing at a place, and all week you have been showing me where that place is. I have wanted the overflow while standing off to the side of it. Put me under the Head. Align my home, align my work, align this church, and let what you poured out on your Son run all the way down until it covers every name in this house. We are yours. Let it flow. Amen.
