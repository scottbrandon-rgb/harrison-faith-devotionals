## Devotional
Paul hands out a command in Ephesians 5:18 and then spends the next three verses telling you what obeying it looks like. Be filled with the Spirit. That is an imperative, and everything after it hangs off of it. Addressing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs. Singing and making melody to the Lord. Giving thanks always and for everything. And then the last one, which is where most of us quietly get off the bus. "Submitting to one another out of reverence for Christ" (Ephesians 5:21).

**Submission is evidence.** Paul sets it in the same breath as singing and thanksgiving, which makes a submitted life proof that the Spirit is in the room. Be honest about what you were like before Christ got hold of you. I can be. I hated authority. I could not stand being told what to do by anybody who hadn't earned it in my eyes, and the Lord had to do something in me before any of this was possible. You weren't submitting on your own either, because you didn't have the power to. That's the tell. Where the Spirit fills, alignment follows.

**The oil that makes you anointed is the same oil that makes you aligned.** Throughout Scripture oil stands for the Spirit, and Paul says the evidence of that oil is a person who submits. So you can't claim the anointing while walking in disobedience to the word that told you to submit. Same jar, same oil.

**Wives.** Paul starts the chain with the wife, and he's writing into a culture where the whole letter was aimed at men, so this turn is deliberate. "Wives, submit to your own husbands, as to the Lord" (Ephesians 5:22). We know you have an opinion. We know you have a preference. We know you have heard from the Lord yourself. The instruction still stands, and everything hangs on those last four words, as to the Lord. The submission is rendered to Christ through the man, which makes it worship before it is ever marriage.

He carries weight for it too. Your husband answers to God for the direction he leads that family, which is why Scripture lays the entrance of sin at Adam's feet even though Eve ate first (Romans 5:12). Headship is accountability long before it is privilege.

**Children.** "Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right" (Ephesians 6:1). Same phrase again, in the Lord. The honor goes up the chain and lands on Christ.

**Parents, your discipline is how they learn it.** The Lord disciplines the one he loves (Hebrews 12:6), so correction is one of the ways love actually shows up in a house. I read a list of hard truths from teachers this week and the first one was this. Teach your children what the word no means so I don't have to. That is where we are. A generation of kids walks into a classroom having never once been told no by somebody who meant it, and they can't honor a person because they were never taught to revere one.

The Lord shows us the other road in Isaiah 5. He sings over Israel as a vineyard he planted and dug and cleared of stones, and when it yields wild grapes he says, "I will make it a waste; it shall not be pruned or hoed" (Isaiah 5:6). His judgment was to quit tending the garden. Discipline is the tending. When correction stops in a home, what is leaving is attention.

**And for those of you whose parents made it hard.** Some of you had a father worth honoring and some of you had the other kind. Mine was the other kind, worse than most of what people in this church have described to me, and the Lord dealt with me about it directly. It is not a matter of his merit. It is a matter of your submission. Honor him wherever his life does not run contrary to the word of God, and where it does, you are released. We will deal with that boundary on Friday.

**People filled with the Spirit are people who line up.**

## Application
1. Where have you been claiming the anointing of God while ignoring something his word plainly told you to submit to?
2. What is one instruction in your home you have been unwilling to give or unwilling to take?

## Challenge
Go to the person you are hardest to submit to, your husband, your mom, your dad, your supervisor, and do one thing today the way they asked instead of the way you prefer. Do it without announcing it and without the speech that usually comes with it. Then tell the Lord you did it as unto him.

## Prayer
Holy Spirit, you filled me so I could live a life I could not live on my own, and submission is the part I keep negotiating. I have wanted the oil without the order. Teach me to render obedience to Christ through the people you put over me, and where I have been the hardest one in my own house, soften me before you have to break me. Amen.
