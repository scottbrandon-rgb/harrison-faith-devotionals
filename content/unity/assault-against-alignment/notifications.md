Sunday we looked at the enemy's two oldest plays against a church that's lined up under Christ, and this week we take them one at a time. Monday we go back to the garden and see that his strategy has never changed. Tuesday takes us to Ephesus, a church that kept the truth and lost the love. Wednesday we go to Thyatira, a church that kept the love and went quiet on the truth. Thursday we land on Ephesians 4:15 and what it looks like to speak the truth in love at home, at church, and at work. Friday we find out how a church stays unified, and it starts with listening to the Word, learning it, and living it.
