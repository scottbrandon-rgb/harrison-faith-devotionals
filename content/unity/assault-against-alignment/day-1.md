## Devotional
Last week we watched the oil run down from the head, over the beard and onto the collar, and we landed on a simple truth. Anointed people are aligned people. The enemy knows that too. He can't take the authority Christ gave you, so he goes after the thing that lets the blessing reach you, and that thing is your alignment. He has run the same play since the garden, and he has never needed a new one because the old one still works.

Watch Paul deal with it in three different cities. To Philippi he writes, "Look out for the dogs, look out for the evildoers, look out for those who mutilate the flesh" (Philippians 3:2). To Galatia he writes, "O foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you?" (Galatians 3:1), and the word bewitched was used for casting the evil eye, a spell that makes you see crooked. To Corinth he goes all the way back to Eden, afraid "that as the serpent deceived Eve by his cunning, your thoughts will be led astray from a sincere and pure devotion to Christ" (2 Corinthians 11:3). Three churches, three sets of false teachers, and one strategy. The serpent walked into Eden with a conversation, and his first move was a question about what God said (Genesis 3:1).

Pay attention to how these teachers sound when they show up. Paul says they deceive "by smooth talk and flattery" (Romans 16:18), and the Greek word for flattery is eulogia, which is the word for blessing. It's the same word the Greek Old Testament uses in Psalm 133:3, "there the LORD has commanded the blessing." The people who pull a church out of alignment usually walk in talking about blessing and how much they love you. Paul wasn't surprised by any of it, "for even Satan disguises himself as an angel of light" (2 Corinthians 11:14). I keep a radar up for people who talk blessing, because people who block blessing talk blessing.

This conversation is preventative, and it's for maturity. We're having it on a quiet Monday, with nothing on fire at Harrison Faith, because this is when a church can actually learn. Paul said "we are not ignorant of his designs" (2 Corinthians 2:11), and you stay that way by learning his designs before they show up at your door. Unity requires truth and love, and this week we'll watch the enemy go after both. He has a strategy for this church, and he has one for your marriage, your family, and your job. It's the same one he used on Philippi, Galatia, and Corinth.

**Satan can't take your authority, so he comes after your alignment.**

## Application
1. Honestly, whose voice in your life sounds like blessing but keeps pulling you away from Christ and his people?
2. What is one habit you could start this week so you know God's voice well enough to catch the serpent's?

## Challenge
Read Genesis 3:1-6 today and watch the serpent work. Count how many times he talks, and notice how he bends what God actually said. Then write down one voice you've been listening to that sounds like blessing, and ask the Lord to show you whether it's pulling you closer to the Head or further from it.

## Prayer
Father, the enemy hasn't changed his strategy, and I don't want to be the one who never saw it coming. Give me ears that can tell the difference between your blessing and a voice that only talks like it. Keep my thoughts from being led astray from a sincere and pure devotion to Christ. Guard this church, guard my home, and keep us lined up under your Son. In Jesus' name, amen.
