## Devotional
Paul wrote Ephesians 4 to the same church Jesus would later correct for leaving its first love, which means Ephesus had the answer in writing years before it drifted. The whole chapter is about unity, "one body and one Spirit... one Lord, one faith, one baptism" (Ephesians 4:4-5), and then Paul tells them where unity is headed. "Until we all attain to the unity of the faith and of the knowledge of the Son of God, to mature manhood" (Ephesians 4:13). Unity is what maturity looks like in a body. The goal is "that we may no longer be children, tossed to and fro by the waves and carried about by every wind of doctrine, by human cunning, by craftiness in deceitful schemes" (Ephesians 4:14).

That word schemes is methodeia, the same word Paul uses two chapters later for "the schemes of the devil" (Ephesians 6:11), and it's kin to our word method. The enemy works a method. He divides us with truth that has no love, and he deceives us with love that has no truth. God answers with one sentence. "Rather, speaking the truth in love, we are to grow up in every way into him who is the head, into Christ" (Ephesians 4:15). In Greek, speaking the truth is a single verb, and you could almost read it as truthing in love. It covers the words that come out of your mouth and the life that backs them up, and Paul sets all of it inside love.

Now bring it home, because that's where alignment gets tested. Right after this, Paul carries the same truth into the house, wives and husbands, then children and parents, then servants and masters, which for us is employees and employers (Ephesians 5:22-6:9). We're aligned vertically with Christ as the head so that we can be aligned horizontally with the people God put next to us. You can't love people without truth. If you try, you'll drift into error right alongside them, and that is deception. People need the truth when they're walking in error, and people need love to help them receive it.

Solomon put it this way. "Faithful are the wounds of a friend; profuse are the kisses of an enemy" (Proverbs 27:6). Judas betrayed Jesus with a kiss (Luke 22:48). A real friend will risk wounding you with the truth because they love you, and an enemy will flatter you all the way into the ditch. Some of you run from confrontation with everything in you, and I understand that. Love will still require it of you, however you're wired. Unity requires truth and love, and the goal is always the person, restored and growing up into Christ.

**Speak the truth, and speak it in love.**

## Application
1. Which comes easier for you, truth or love, and who in your home has been paying for the one you skip?
2. Who is one person in your marriage, family, church, or workplace you need to speak the truth to this week, and how will you make sure they feel your love when you do?

## Challenge
Before you have a hard conversation this week, write down two lines. On the first, write the truth you need to say, and on the second, write the love you want them to feel while you say it. If you can't fill in both lines, you aren't ready yet, so pray until you can.

## Prayer
Lord Jesus, you are the Head, and you came full of grace and truth. Teach me to speak the truth in love in my marriage, my family, my church, and my work. Where I've been harsh, make me gentle, and where I've been silent, make me honest. Grow me up into you so this body stays joined together under your authority. In Jesus' name, amen.
