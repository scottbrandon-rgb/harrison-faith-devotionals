## Devotional
Paul closes his warning in Romans 16 with a compliment. "For your obedience is known to all, so that I rejoice over you" (Romans 16:19). He opened the letter the same way, thanking God that their "faith is proclaimed in all the world" (Romans 1:8). Their faith was strong because their obedience was strong, and Paul knew that was their defense. Unity is maintained through obedience to God's Word. The Greek word for obedience is hupakoe, built from two pieces, under and hear. To obey is to hear from underneath, which is alignment all over again, a people standing under the Head and listening.

Think about a bank teller. She handles real bills all day long, so when a counterfeit slides across the counter she can feel it before she can explain it. The more you handle the real thing, the quicker you spot the fake. Listen to it, because "faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ" (Romans 10:17), which is why being in the room when the Word is preached matters. Learn it, because most of us hold on to about 10 percent of what we hear, and Peter tells us to "grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ" (2 Peter 3:18). Then you've got to live it. "Be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves" (James 1:22). A hearer who never does anything with the Word deceives himself, and the enemy doesn't even have to show up.

Last Saturday we celebrated the people who serve this church, and it was a picture of this whole week. Serving is the best way to get connected at Harrison Faith, and it's the best way to walk out what you hear on Sunday. You can't serve next to somebody for long without learning to tell them the truth and love them at the same time, and that's the unity we've been after all week. If you listen well, learn well, and live well, you won't be easy prey.

Then read the very next thing Paul writes. "The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet" (Romans 16:20). That is Genesis 3:15 coming due. The serpent who deceived Eve by his cunning is going to have his head crushed, and God is going to do it under the feet of an obedient, unified church. He is the God of peace, and he is the one doing the crushing. The enemy has a strategy, and God has the last word.

**Listen to it, learn it, live it.**

## Application
1. Honestly, which is weakest in your life right now, listening to the Word, learning it, or living it?
2. What is one place you could serve at Harrison Faith this month that would put what you heard this week into practice?

## Challenge
Pick one passage from this week and read it every day for the next seven days, until you could explain it to someone without looking. Then find one place to serve at Harrison Faith and let the church know you're ready to start. The Word you live is the Word you keep.

## Prayer
Father, the God of peace, thank you that on the same day the serpent spoke in the garden, you promised to crush his head. Give me ears that hear from underneath you, a mind that keeps learning, and hands that do what your Word says. Keep this church wise as to what is good and innocent as to what is evil. Crush the enemy under our feet, and let the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with us. In Jesus' name, amen.
