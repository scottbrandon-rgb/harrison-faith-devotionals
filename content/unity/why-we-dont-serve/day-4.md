## Devotional
Jesus told a story about a servant coming in from the field. He'd been plowing all day or chasing sheep across a hillside, and when he walked through the door his master didn't pull out a chair for him. The master said, "Prepare supper for me, and dress properly, and serve me while I eat and drink, and afterward you will eat and drink" (Luke 17:8). The word Jesus used for servant is doulos, a bondservant, a man whose whole day belonged to someone else.

Most of us read that and side with the servant, because it feels inconsiderate. If you've served in a church for very long, you've felt that sting. Someone leaves a mess after the class party. Someone forgets to plan ahead and needs it done right now. Someone asks why the coffee is cold, and someone else walks past the person who set up every chair in the room without a word. Inconsiderate people will test your loyalty faster than almost anything.

Jesus aimed this story at the servant's attitude while he serves. Paul gives the reason that attitude can hold: "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men... You are serving the Lord Christ" (Colossians 3:23-24). The word heartily literally means from the soul. When you serve the body, you submit to the Head, and when you submit to the Head, you serve the body (Ephesians 4:15-16).

Years ago I was setting up a room for Sunday at another church. We had just moved buildings, it was Saturday night, and I was there by myself. By 3:30 in the morning I was worn out and convinced no one knew or cared what I was doing. I rode the lift down from the top of the truss, ready to go home and grab 2 hours of sleep, and realized I'd left a safety cable unclipped on one of the lights. It had nothing to do with whether the light worked, and no one would ever see it.

So I rode back up. The whole way to the top I kept asking, who's going to see this, who cares what I'm doing up here? The Lord reminded me on that lift that he sees everything, and the reason I was clipping a cable no one would ever see was that my loyalty was to him. "For God is not unjust so as to overlook your work and the love that you have shown for his name in serving the saints" (Hebrews 6:10). Inconsiderate people can't cancel your reward, because they were never the ones keeping the books.

**Serve like the Head is watching, because he is.**

## Application
1. Who has been inconsiderate toward your serving lately, and how has it changed the way you show up?
2. What is one unseen task you could do this week purely as service to Jesus?

## Challenge
Do one job this week that no one will ever notice, and finish it all the way down to the last cable. Don't mention it to anyone, not even your spouse. When you're done, tell the Lord, "This one was for you."

## Prayer
Lord Jesus, you see the work done at 3:30 in the morning and the work done in a back room no one walks past. Forgive me for letting inconsiderate people decide how faithfully I serve. Make my loyalty to you steadier than my frustration with them. When I serve your body, remind me that I'm serving you. Amen.
