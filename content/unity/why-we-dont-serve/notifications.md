Sunday we saw that a body only works when every part serves the others, and then we asked the harder question of why so many of us still don't. Monday we start in 1 Corinthians 12 with God's design for a body where every member has a job. Tuesday and Wednesday we take on insecurity from both sides, the kind that thinks it's too big to serve and the kind that thinks it's too small. Thursday we deal with the inconsiderate people who test our loyalty, and Friday we face ingratitude by keeping our eyes on what Jesus paid.
