## Devotional
If you have felt worn thin lately, you are not imagining it, because grief keeps no schedule that any of us gets to set. It arrives late, it stays long, and it shows up again on a Monday for no reason all. Daniel watched an enemy who understood all of that about God's people centuries before we ever experienced ourselves.

The angel tells Daniel what this power will do, and the verb is worth slowing down over. He "shall wear out the saints of the Most High" (Daniel 7:25). That word carries the same picture Scripture uses for clothing gone threadbare, the cloaks and sandals that somehow lasted 40 years in the wilderness because God kept them whole (Deuteronomy 29:5). No one tears a shirt that way, it thins at the elbow through years of ordinary use, and then one morning you put it on and it gives.

Once a people is worn that thin, division comes cheap. Jesus said it to the Pharisees who accused him of casting out demons by Beelzebul: "Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and no city or house divided against itself will stand" (Matthew 12:25). Even the kingdom of darkness keeps its ranks, because a house at war with itself never survives long enough to do real damage.

So the darkness holds a standard and that ought to trouble us, because the church has been known to live beneath a line the devil manages to keep. An enemy who cannot split us with one blow will happily take weeks of funerals and let exhaustion do the work for him.

We are giving 5 days to unity because what this church has is worth guarding. This body is whole. You have buried friends together, filled hospital rooms together, and carried each other through a stretch that would have wrecked others. Guard it now, while you still have it to guard, because the enemy in Daniel does his best work on people too tired to notice they are drifting, and because a body that stays together is the proof this world has that the Father sent the Son.

**Guard the unity you already have, because tired people give ground they never meant to give.**

## Application
1. Where has this season worn you thin enough that you have quietly stopped reaching for the people who love you?
2. Who is one person in this church you could call this week just to tell them you are still here?

## Challenge
Pick one person who has walked through a funeral in the last 6 weeks and make contact today. Even a text message counts. Say the name of the one they lost, tell them you have been thinking about them, and then stay on the line long enough to hear how they actually are.

## Prayer
Father, I am tired, and I am saying that out loud to you this morning. You saw this coming when you showed Daniel an enemy who wears your people down over time, and you see it now in me. Keep me close to the people you put around me in this season. Hold this church together while we are worn, and let our staying together say something true about your Son to everyone watching. Amen.
