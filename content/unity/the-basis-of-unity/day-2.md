## Devotional
A transplant patient takes medication for the rest of his life for one simple reason, his body can spot a fake. When it reads the DNA of the new organ, it detects a stranger, and then goes after it. So the whole reason medication is there to tell the body to ignore the foreign DNA so the organ can be accepted. Your DNA matters. 

Paul reaches for that same illustration. "For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so it is with Christ" (1 Corinthians 12:12).

Then he tells you what makes those members one. "For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body ... and all were made to drink of one Spirit" (1 Corinthians 12:13). Jews and Greeks, slaves and free, all of them handed the same Spirit.

The Holy Spirit is to a believer what DNA is to a body. He is the marker that says this one is ours. You were never built to run on two sets of DNA, physically or spiritually, which is why a life split between the Spirit and the flesh always feels like a war going on inside.

That also explains something you have felt for years and maybe never understood how to explain it. I’m willing to bet, there are rooms where you do not fit. The job, the old friend group, a family table at Thanksgiving, and sometimes, painfully, a church.

The discomfort runs both directions and it is closer to chemistry than to personality. You carry a marker they do not, and something in them reads it. Jesus told his disciples the world would hate them for that exact reason (John 15:19).

The same marker that makes you foreign in some rooms makes you family in this ONE. 

Ima… say that again, the same spiritual DNA that makes you foreign in some rooms makes you family in this ONE.

You did not audition for this body and you cannot be voted out of it, because the Spirit who placed you here is the one who holds you here. Your seat does not depend on your usefulness, your attendance record, or how you performed in the last 6 weeks.

Paul echoes this in Ephesians. "There is one body and one Spirit ... one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all" (Ephesians 4:4-6).

That is seven ones in three verses. Count them the next time somebody tells you the church is whatever we decide to make it.

**The Spirit who marked you as God's,
marked the person next to you the same way.**

## Application
1. Where have you treated your place in this church like something you earned and could lose?
2. What would change in how you speak to one person here if you actually believed the Spirit assigned them to you?

## Challenge
Find someone in this church you have quietly written off as too different from you, and go sit near them Sunday. You do not have to explain yourself or fix anything between you. Ask one real question about their week.

## Prayer
Father, thank you for putting your Spirit in me and settling the question of where I belong before I ever had a vote in it. Forgive me for the times I have treated this church like a preference I could set down. Teach me to see the marker you placed in the people around me, especially the ones I find hard to be near. Keep me from trying to run on anything other than your Spirit. Amen.
